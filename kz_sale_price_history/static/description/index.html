Sales Price History

Track and review historical sales prices in Odoo 19

Easily access the historical sales prices of products from previous sales orders. View useful pricing information such as the customer, sales order, order date, and unit price from one convenient history view. Built by Kogzio Solutions for Odoo Community and Enterprise.

Overview

Sales Price History provides a quick way to review previously used sales prices for products directly from sales orders.

It helps users understand how a product was priced in earlier orders by showing the related customer, sales order number, order date, and unit price. The history can be filtered by the current sales order customer, or the customer filter can be removed to review all historical prices for the product.

The history is available directly from the sales order line through a dedicated Price History button, allowing users to check historical pricing without leaving the sales workflow.

Key Features

How It Works

  • Open a Sales Order and add a product to the order line.
  • Open the product's sales order line.
  • Click the Price History button.
  • The system displays previous sales price records for that product with the current customer filter applied by default.
  • Remove the customer filter to view all historical price records for the product.
  • Review the customer, sales order, order date, and unit price.

Historical Pricing Information

  • Previous sales order reference
  • Customer associated with the order
  • Customer-specific filtering of price history
  • Sales order date
  • Historical unit price
