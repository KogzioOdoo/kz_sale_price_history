Stock Risk Analysis

Identify inventory risks and make better stock decisions in Odoo 19

Analyze inventory risk based on stock quantities, recent sales activity, and product history. The module helps identify Slow Movers, Dead Stock, and Overstock Risk directly from the Inventory reporting workflow. Built by Kogzio Solutions for Odoo Community and Enterprise.

Overview

Stock Risk Analysis provides a dedicated reporting view for identifying products that may require inventory attention.

The report evaluates products using their available stock, recent sales quantity, last sale activity, and product creation date when no sales history is available.

Each identified product is assigned a risk status and a dynamically generated description explaining the reason for the classification.

Key Features

  • Identify Slow Mover products
  • Identify Dead Stock products
  • Identify Overstock Risk products
  • Display current units on hand
  • Track recent sales quantity
  • Track the last sold date
  • Use product creation date when no sale exists
  • Dynamic risk descriptions
  • Dedicated Stock Risk Analysis reporting view
  • Available from Inventory → Reporting

Risk Categories

  • Dead Stock: Products with stock on hand and no sales or activity for 365 days or more.
  • Slow Mover: Products with stock on hand and no sales or activity for 30 days or more, but less than 365 days.
  • Overstock Risk: Products with more than 100 units on hand and sales of 1% or less of the current units on hand during the last 90 days.

Dynamic Risk Description

The Description field dynamically explains why a product has been classified as a particular risk.

  • Example: No sales for 35 days
  • Example: No sales for 97 days
  • Example: 1500.00 units on hand and only 3.00 units sold in the last 90 days

How It Works

  1. Open the Inventory application.
  2. Go to Reporting.
  3. Select Stock Risk Analysis.
  4. Review the products identified as Slow Mover, Dead Stock, or Overstock Risk.
  5. Review units on hand, description, and status for each product.

Report Information

  • Product
  • Units On Hand
  • Sales Quantity in the last 90 days
  • Dynamic Description
  • Risk Status
